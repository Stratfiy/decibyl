BEFORE YOU RECHARGE

Understand the proposed model.

How much is one credit?＋

The proposed model defines one credit as ₹1 of usage at Decibyl’s published retail rate card, not ₹1 of raw provider expense. Different models and jobs consume different amounts.

Is there a limit on the number of bots?＋

The proposed plans do not charge per bot or workflow. Simultaneous execution, storage, provider limits, and usage budgets still apply.

How many calls will 999 credits cover?＋

There is no universal conversion. Language models, voice generation, conversation length, call destination, and transfers affect usage. Request an estimate for the selected setup.

Can I purchase credits on this website?＋

Not yet. This is a launch-pricing preview with a demo-booking route, not a payment checkout. Final commercial terms and billing availability must be confirmed.

Does the plan include personal setup and custom integration?＋

Not unlimited hands-on services. Basic product guidance and separately scoped implementation should be agreed before deployment.

Can I test without purchasing a phone number?＋

Browser voice and text testing do not require a telephone number. Phone calling needs a supported carrier and number setup.
