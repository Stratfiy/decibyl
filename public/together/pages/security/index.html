CONTROLS IN THE PLATFORM

Practical protection, built around conversations.

01

Private media access

Recordings and transcripts are accessed using expiring signed links rather than permanent public media URLs. Private storage configuration remains part of deployment.

02

Configurable retention

Set separate account retention windows for recordings and transcripts. The application includes scheduled deletion processing.

03

Export and erasure

Authenticated account-scoped tools support call-data export and erasure requests for a phone number. Financial records and other retention scopes require separate treatment.

04

Access history

The platform records issuance of signed media links and exports. This tells you when access was granted; it does not prove that a file was played or downloaded.

05

Credential protection

The implementation includes encrypted provider and carrier secret storage, dependent on correctly configured deployment keys. Secrets are not the same as provider account identifiers.

06

Organization-scoped access

Core account routes scope requests to the selected organization. Owner-only membership management exists; fine-grained restrictions across every product action are not claimed.

07

Recording and outreach controls

Recording disclosure, campaign consent attestation, and do-not-call controls support your process. The business still needs an appropriate basis and permission to contact people.

08

Provider visibility

Account-scoped processing information identifies configured vendors and providers involved in calls. Review the actual provider path for your selected setup.

RETENTION THAT MATCHES THE PURPOSE

Audio and text do not need
to stay for the same time.

DEFAULT RECORDING WINDOW90 days

Audio retention can be configured per account.

DEFAULT TRANSCRIPT WINDOW365 days

Transcript retention has its own setting.

These are application defaults, not a legal recommendation. Confirm your configured policy, deletion jobs, backups, connected systems, and provider retention before rollout.

PRIVACY & PROCESSING

Choose the data path deliberately.

Speech, language, telephony, storage, and connected apps may process different parts of a conversation.

↗

Check the provider terms.

◎

Agree the deployment scope.

EVIDENCE BEFORE BADGES

A security conversation you can actually have.

Is Decibyl SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certified?＋

No SOC 2 report or ISO 27001 certificate is claimed in the reviewed product documentation. These should not be assumed from the presence of technical controls.

Can I send protected health information through Decibyl?＋

The reviewed documentation does not establish HIPAA BAAs for Decibyl and all relevant sub-processors. Do not use this site as assurance that regulated health data is supported.

Is all data guaranteed to stay in India?＋

No blanket residency promise is made here. The hosting location and third-party speech, language, and app processing must be confirmed for the specific deployment.

Do all members have different fine-grained permissions?＋

The current organization roles include owner-controlled membership changes, but members retain broad product access. Do not assume role-by-role restrictions for every workflow, call, or setting.

Do technical controls make us automatically compliant?＋

No. Applicable obligations also involve lawful processing, contracts, notices, consent where required, provider terms, and operating procedures. Review these for your business.

How do I report a security issue?＋

Email security@decibyl.ai with a description and safe reproduction steps. Avoid including other customers’ data or publishing the issue before it has been reviewed.
