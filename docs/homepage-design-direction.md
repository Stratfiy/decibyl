# Decibyl early-access homepage

Primary reference: Wajo homepage, reviewed 6 October 2026. Preserve the bright white canvas, centred confident headline, playful floating composition, understandable task examples and short calls to action. Adapt to both personal and business work.

Refero sources:
- Qatchup, style 3b41db61-34e3-40cb-b9ee-292683029b53: floating, softly elevated task cards; generous whitespace.
- Tally, style 32129d0a-39b5-4bbd-8446-fa564f7ff15e: friendly illustration personality. Its blue CTA system is not imported.
- Magicbeans, style 47d378ed-249d-4646-8789-60918628aafe: neutral pill actions and restrained product framing.

| Decision | Source | Application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| White canvas and black typography | Wajo | Whole homepage |
| Dark pill CTA | Wajo / Magicbeans | Waitlist navigation and primary action |
| Floating task cards | Qatchup | Hero composition, subtle shadows |
| Friendly original bot motif | Wajo's approachable personality, adapted | Code-native small lime character; no copied assets |
| Green completion cues | Product semantics | Demo progress and completion |
| Personal and business examples | Founder brief | Switchable demo and two audience panels |
| Free, invite-only access | Founder brief | Join waitlist, approval, invitation, activation |

Illustrative demos are explicitly labelled. Existing /api/leads persistence, rate limiting, attribution and notification wiring are retained. Waitlist audience is saved in the existing optional vertical field. No production submissions are sent during verification.
