ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW

A planned delivery-term comparison

  1. Select the approved requirement: delivery within 30 calendar days.
  2. Read a supplier quotation stating delivery within 45 calendar days.
  3. Flag the 15-day difference, cite both sources, and draft a clarification for the buyer.
THE RESULT TO REVIEW

Requirement: 30 days. Offered: 45 days. Difference: 15 days. Action: buyer review.

Illustrative arithmetic using invented sample terms. No document is uploaded or processed on this page.

QUESTIONS BEFORE THE FIRST RUN

Know what you are putting to work.

Can I upload documents here?＋

No. This is a product-direction page, not a live upload tool. Book a scoping session to discuss a document-analysis pilot.

How does this differ from the existing knowledge feature?＋

The existing knowledge capability supports questions over configured source material. End-to-end comparison, deviation review, and report-generation job templates described here are planned.

Will it always detect every deviation?＋

No such guarantee is made. A useful implementation needs explicit requirements, source evidence, evaluation examples, and human review of uncertain findings.

Will my documents train other customers’ agents?＋

This page makes no cross-customer training or blanket provider-retention claim. Processing terms, access, retention, and the selected model providers must be confirmed for your deployment.
