WHAT IS AN AI AGENT?

Instructions, context, and a way to act.

✳

A job to carry out

An agent combines instructions with a model and tools to perform a defined task. Useful agents also need boundaries, tests, and a way to handle exceptions.

◎

A result you can inspect

An answer, a structured update, or an action should be traceable to the information and tools used. Autonomy should match the job and the available evidence.

QUESTIONS ABOUT AGENTS

Start with a clear expectation.

Can AI agents work without a phone number?＋

Yes. Non-calling workflows and text or browser testing do not require a telephone number. Calling adds a supported carrier setup and associated costs.

Does every agent need to be built from scratch?＋

No. Templates provide reusable instructions and workflow structure. Your job rules, tools, and knowledge are configured for your situation.

Can I use Decibyl like Slack or Grok Bot?＋

Decibyl is developing a shared workspace for people and agents. The current site illustrates that direction; it does not claim complete feature equivalence or a live collaboration workspace.

Will installing more bots make my bill larger?＋

The proposed credit model does not charge per bot. Running jobs, AI usage, phone calls, and relevant capacity or storage consume resources. Confirm your pilot’s rate card before use.
