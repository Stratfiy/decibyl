QUESTIONS BEFORE THE FIRST RUN

Know what you are putting to work.

What can a customer-support AI agent do?＋

It can interpret an enquiry, retrieve relevant information, and use authorised tools to carry out a defined support task. The actions available depend on your connected systems and configuration.

Is this a help desk replacement?＋

Decibyl can connect agents to an existing support process. A full ticketing system, every help-desk integration, and every messaging channel should not be assumed; scope the required connections first.

Can support agents handle phone calls?＋

The voice platform supports configured inbound and outbound calls, browser voice testing, and transfer paths. Carrier setup and language/model selection affect the experience.

How should we measure a support pilot?＋

Track correct resolutions, transfers, repeat contacts, review effort, and cost per completed enquiry. A short conversation is not automatically a resolved issue.
