# Character reference

`decibyl-operator-reference.png` is the canonical picture of the recurring
Decibyl operator: cream blazer, coral shirt, charcoal trousers, white trainers,
dark wavy shoulder-length hair.

**Every future room render must pass this file as `image_input`.**

It exists because the set drifted. The plates were conditioned on whichever
earlier plate happened to be to hand, and for several runs that was the commerce
floor — which contains no cream-blazer operator at all. With nothing to copy,
each render invented her again: the clinic came back with her as a doctor in a
white coat, and the commerce and call-floor plates dropped her entirely.

It is the hero front-desk render rather than a cropped figure on purpose. A
tight crop of a person conditions the model toward producing a portrait; a whole
room with her clearly in it carries the character *and* the room grammar — the
camera angle, the cutaway walls, the plinth, the palette — which is the rest of
what has to stay consistent.

Downscaled to 1280px. Conditioning does not benefit from the full 2752px master,
and a 4.5MB PNG does not belong in a marketing repository.
