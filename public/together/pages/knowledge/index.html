THREE KINDS OF CONTEXT

An answer. A memory. A relationship.

▤

Company knowledge

Upload supported documents, process their content, and ground answers in retrieved material. Weak matches should prompt clarification.

◷

Shared and bot memory

Keep durable facts at organization or bot scope. Trace a remembered fact to its source and prefer your own records over inferred information.

◎

Enterprise knowledge graph

Capture relationships through optional graph infrastructure. It needs a configured graph service; it is not a guarantee that every app or company record is connected.

THE BOUNDARIES MATTER

Useful context needs a clear scope.

Does the knowledge graph connect all our systems automatically?＋

No. The implementation includes optional graph infrastructure and supported ingestion paths. Your connected sources and graph deployment must be scoped and validated for your use case.

Is every remembered fact automatically correct?＋

No. Inferred facts can be wrong. Decibyl keeps provenance and separates inferred memory from your own records so the source can be reviewed.

How does this relate to deletion?＋

Call-content retention and erasure controls exist. Before rollout, review the scope for documents, backups, external providers, and any graph-derived data as part of your deployment’s processing setup.
