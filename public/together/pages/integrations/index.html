CHOOSE THE CONNECTION

A route for each kind of system.

01

App connections through Composio

Connect supported app toolkits and authorize the actions your bot needs. Availability depends on the toolkit, account permissions, and configuration.

02

Your existing n8n workflows

Use an HTTP or webhook connection to call the workflows you already maintain. This is an integration pattern, not a separate native n8n editor.

03

Custom HTTP tools

Call a business API with configured inputs and authentication. Bespoke systems need setup and end-to-end validation.

04

MCP tools

Attach compatible MCP tool servers and use Decibyl’s own MCP surface to inspect, create, and edit workflows.

05

Webhooks and SDKs

Trigger calls or receive workflow data in your backend. Python and TypeScript SDKs support developer-led integration.

06

Telephony and speech providers

Bring supported carrier credentials and choose the speech and language providers that fit your required languages and use case.
